description: Equal-height cards that pin and stack as their wrapper scrolls.
overview: 'A wrapper for cards in one track, each pinned a little lower than the last, so scrolling slides every card up over the one before it and the covered cards show as paper edges above. On desktop, each new card begins below the viewport so the first card gets a clear opening moment. Every card matches the natural height of the tallest card, has a subtle border, and the track includes enough trailing space for the last card to finish stacking. A rail of anchor links marks the card on top. Place it inside a page section or another wide wrapper. Below 768px nothing pins, the rail is hidden, and the cards stack flat.'
