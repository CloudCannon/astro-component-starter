## External media

YouTube, Vimeo, maps and embeds load straight away by default. Turn on **Ask before loading external media** in **Privacy & Consent** and they wait for the visitor's permission instead, with a prompt in their place and a switch in the settings dialog. Turn it on for any site with visitors in the EU or UK.

## Before launch

Rewrite the privacy policy at `/privacy/` for your site. After a significant policy change, increase the policy revision so visitors are asked again. See the [privacy guide](https://github.com/CloudCannon/astro-component-starter/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md) for setup and testing.
