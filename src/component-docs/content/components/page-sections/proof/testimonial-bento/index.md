overview: 'Quotes laid out as a bento grid. Each quote picks a size (standard, wide, tall, featured, or full width) and a tone, and smaller quotes fill the gaps left by bigger ones, so a few short quotes can sit around one strong one. Use [Testimonial Wall](/component-docs/components/page-sections/proof/testimonial-wall/) instead for many quotes of similar weight. The grid is container-queried: it drops to two columns, then one, as the space narrows, and on small screens every quote is full width.'
